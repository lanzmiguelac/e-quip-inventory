# E-QUIP presentation script

Use fictional people and equipment only. The site is initially owner-private, so a presenter must pass the site's access gate before using an application account. Sharing with panelists is a separate access choice.

1. Sign in as the administrator. Show dashboard counts, inventory search/filtering, and an equipment record. Register an asset with a unique asset number, location, and optional photo.
2. Refresh or open a separate browser session to demonstrate that records come from the shared database.
3. Sign in as student@demo.equip.school. Request an Available asset for today, with today or a later date as the due date. Show that Users and Reports are unavailable to borrowers.
4. Sign in as staff@demo.equip.school. Open the request and approve it. Show that inventory marks the asset Reserved immediately.
5. A competing approval or release for this asset must fail. This is enforced by the database, including when two staff act simultaneously.
6. Open the approved request and choose Release. Record condition and accessories. Show the new transaction and Borrowed status.
7. In Transactions, record a Good return. Show that the asset becomes Available and the handover remains in the history.
8. Repeat with another asset and return it as Needs Repair. Describe the damage. Show the automatic maintenance record and unavailable status. Resolve the repair with a cost and confirmed Good/Fair condition.
9. Generate a QR label. Download/print it, and scan it on a phone after confirming HTTPS camera permissions. If camera access is unavailable, use a saved QR image or the manual asset lookup.
10. Show recipient notifications, CSV exports, and recent activity. Show account creation or role management as administrator. Passwords are reset, never displayed.

Philippine Time is Asia/Manila, UTC+8. Due dates include the entire selected school-local day. An overdue report lists only open loans whose due dates have passed; generating it does not alter records.

Avoid using real student identities. Do not present the demo accounts as production-ready school accounts. Change the initial demo passwords if you share application access beyond the presenter.
